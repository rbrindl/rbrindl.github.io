# Corfu (Kerkyra) – Paxos – Antipaxos Sailing Itinerary

**Duration:** Sunday morning to Friday evening  
**Start:** Gouvia Marina, Kerkyra (Corfu)  
**Return:** Gouvia Marina, Kerkyra (Corfu)  
**Yacht:** Sailing yacht with approximately 2 m draft  
**Main interests:** Swimming stops, attractive harbour towns, one possible anchorage night, short walks and historical or scenic shore visits

> Corfu is known in Greek as **Κέρκυρα** and is commonly transliterated as **Kerkyra** or **Kérkyra**. Nautical sources use both “Corfu” and “Kerkyra”.

## Route overview

| Day | Route | Overnight | Approx. sailing |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Sunday | Gouvia → Paxos, with a swimming stop near Petriti or south-east Corfu | Gaios | 28–32 NM |
| Monday | Gaios → Antipaxos | Antipaxos at anchor, weather permitting | 8–12 NM |
| Tuesday | Antipaxos → Lakka | Lakka | 12–16 NM |
| Wednesday | Lakka → Sivota/Syvota | Sivota | 15–20 NM |
| Thursday | Sivota → Corfu Town | Corfu Town | 20–25 NM |
| Friday | Corfu Town → Gouvia | Return to marina | 5–8 NM |

The previous night is spent in Corfu Town, so the itinerary does not use Corfu Town as the first overnight stop.

---

## Sunday — Gouvia to Gaios, Paxos

### Sailing plan

Leave Gouvia in the morning and sail south toward Paxos. This is the longest passage of the trip, so plan one substantial swimming stop rather than several short stops.

Possible swimming stops include:

- [Petriti, Corfu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petriti,_Corfu)
- Messonghi
- A suitable bay along the south-east coast of Corfu

Continue to [Gaios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaios), the principal harbour of Paxos.

### Overnight

Stay in the town harbour or on an official mooring. With a yacht drawing approximately 2 m:

- Use the marked or main approach channel.
- Avoid uncharted or shallow side areas around the islets.
- Confirm the depth of the assigned berth or mooring with the harbourmaster or charter base.
- Do not rely only on visual water colour to judge depth.

### Land programme

Gaios is compact and easy to explore on foot:

- Walk along the waterfront.
- Explore the narrow streets behind the harbour.
- Visit the small churches and local shops.
- Have dinner overlooking the channel.

### Fallback

If the yacht cannot leave Gouvia early enough for a comfortable passage, spend Sunday night at Petriti and continue to Gaios on Monday. In that case, replace the Antipaxos overnight with Mongonissi or Gaios.

---

## Monday — Gaios to Antipaxos

### Sailing and swimming

Leave Gaios in the morning and sail to [Antipaxos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipaxos).

Spend most of the day swimming and snorkeling around:

- Voutoumi
- Vrika
- Mesovrika

### Draft considerations

For a yacht with a 2 m draft, Antipaxos should primarily be treated as a daytime swimming destination.

Anchor only after checking:

- Actual depth beneath the keel
- Holding quality
- Swinging room
- Wind direction and possible overnight shifts
- Distance from beaches, rocks and other yachts

Do not anchor close to the beach merely because other boats are there. The clear water can make shallow areas difficult to judge visually.

### Overnight

Stay at anchor overnight only if:

- The forecast is settled.
- There is ample depth beneath the keel.
- The anchor has good holding.
- The yacht has adequate swinging room.
- No significant overnight wind shift or swell is expected.

If conditions are uncertain, use one of these alternatives:

- [Mongonissi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paxos)
- Gaios
- Another sheltered Paxos anchorage confirmed locally

---

## Tuesday — Antipaxos to Lakka

### Sailing plan

Enjoy a final morning swim at Antipaxos, then sail north to [Lakka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakka,_Paxos).

If conditions permit, make a short swimming stop in one of the deeper coves along the east coast of Paxos.

### Overnight

Stay in the central part of Lakka Bay or use a mooring whose depth has been confirmed for a 2 m-draft yacht.

Avoid taking the yacht close to:

- Sandy beaches
- Shallow bay edges
- Unmarked side coves
- Areas occupied by small boats or swimmers

### Land programme

Allow approximately 45–60 minutes for an easy walk:

1. Walk around the waterfront and village.
2. Follow the coastal path toward the northern headland.
3. Turn back before the path becomes steep or exposed.
4. Return to the harbour for dinner.

Lakka is a particularly pleasant evening stop because the village is compact and immediately accessible from the harbour.

---

## Wednesday — Lakka to Sivota/Syvota

### Sailing and swimming

Sail toward mainland Greece and stop for a swim around the [Sivota Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sivota,_Thesprotia).

Possible swimming areas include:

- Blue Lagoon
- Pisina Beach
- Sheltered coves around the Sivota Islands

Approach beach anchorages conservatively. They can become shallow quickly, and the turquoise water is not a reliable indication of depth.

### Overnight

Use the main Sivota harbour or a locally recommended deeper mooring.

For a 2 m draft:

- Do not assume every cove is suitable.
- Check the depth continuously while approaching.
- Avoid taking the yacht far into shallow bays.
- Ask the harbourmaster or charter base where deeper yachts normally stay.

### Land programme

Sivota is suitable for a short evening visit:

- Walk along the waterfront.
- Explore the harbour and surrounding lanes.
- Have dinner beside the water.

The walk can easily be kept below one hour.

### Fallback

If Sivota is crowded or uncomfortable in the prevailing wind, use Petriti as an alternative overnight stop.

---

## Thursday — Sivota to Corfu Town

### Sailing and swimming

Return north toward Corfu. Suitable swimming stops include:

- Petriti
- Messonghi
- Benitses
- A deeper cove south of Corfu Town

Allow enough time to arrive in [Corfu Town](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/978/) before evening.

### Overnight

Spend the final night in Corfu Town rather than at anchor. This provides a comfortable margin before returning the yacht to Gouvia on Friday.

### Land programme

Choose one of the following short programmes.

#### Option 1: Old Town walk

- Spianada
- Liston
- Venetian lanes
- Palace of St Michael and St George
- Exterior of the Old Fortress

#### Option 2: Fortress and waterfront

Walk from the harbour toward the Old Fortress and continue along the waterfront. The route can be shortened easily if the crew is tired.

#### Option 3: Dinner in the old town

Enjoy a final dinner near Liston or in the narrow streets behind the main square.

Corfu’s Old Town is a fortified Mediterranean port with Venetian fortifications and neoclassical buildings. Its historic centre is a UNESCO World Heritage site.

---

## Friday — Corfu Town to Gouvia

### Sailing plan

This is a short final passage. Leave Corfu Town after breakfast and make one final swimming stop around:

- Kommeno Bay
- Dassia
- Another suitable bay on the north-east coast

Return to Gouvia Marina by Friday evening.

### Allow time for

- Refuelling
- Returning equipment
- Cleaning and checkout
- Possible delays from wind, traffic or marina congestion

## Draft and anchoring guidelines

For a yacht with approximately 2 m draft:

- Prefer planned anchoring depths of roughly 4–5 m, subject to local advice and the yacht’s actual keel clearance.
- Remember that the depth sounder may measure from the transducer rather than the bottom of the keel.
- Check the yacht’s actual loaded draft, including rudder and propeller.
- Leave extra clearance for swell, swinging and charting inaccuracies.
- Do not rely on water colour to estimate depth.
- Use current electronic charts and a local pilot guide.
- Confirm berth and mooring depths with the charter company.
- Treat Antipaxos, beach anchorages and small harbours as weather- and depth-dependent.
- If conditions deteriorate, favour Gaios, Lakka, Sivota’s main harbour, Petriti or Corfu Town.

## Recommended overnight pattern

1. **Sunday:** Gaios, Paxos
2. **Monday:** Antipaxos at anchor, only if conditions are settled
3. **Tuesday:** Lakka, Paxos
4. **Wednesday:** Sivota/Syvota, mainland Greece
5. **Thursday:** Corfu Town
6. **Friday:** Return to Gouvia Marina

This route provides:

- A dedicated Antipaxos swimming day
- Swimming stops on every sailing day
- Three attractive harbour evenings: Gaios, Lakka and Corfu Town
- One mainland stop at Sivota
- One possible anchorage night
- A short and manageable final passage to Gouvia

## Terminology

The island commonly called Corfu is known in Greek as **Κέρκυρα** and is transliterated as **Kerkyra** or **Kérkyra**.

Sivota may also appear as:

- Sivota
- Syvota
- Σύβοτα

The mainland stop in this itinerary is **Sivota/Syvota in Thesprotia**, not Sivota on Lefkada.
